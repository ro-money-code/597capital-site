01 / About

An independent, research-driven trading firm.

597 Capital is a proprietary trading firm based in New York. We apply quantitative, short-horizon strategies to event contracts, prediction markets, and digital assets, trading exclusively our own capital.

The firm is built on a software and data-engineering foundation: every idea starts as a testable hypothesis, is evaluated against data, and is only deployed through automated systems with explicit risk controls.

02 / Approach

From data to execution, end to end.

  1. 01

    Research

    Hypothesis-driven study of market microstructure, pricing behavior, and order flow, grounded in clean, well-instrumented data.

  2. 02

    Models

    Statistical and machine-learning models built for short horizons, validated out of sample and monitored continuously in production.

  3. 03

    Execution

    Automated, low-latency trading infrastructure engineered in-house, with careful attention to fills, fees, and venue mechanics.

  4. 04

    Risk

    Position limits, exposure controls, and automated safeguards are designed in from the start, not bolted on afterward.
